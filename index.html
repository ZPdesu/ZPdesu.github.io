Peihao Zhu

Senior Applied Scientist · Adobe Firefly

I am a Senior Applied Scientist on the Adobe Firefly team, where I focus on long-video generation — pushing the visual and temporal consistency, controllability, and efficiency of video foundation models beyond the short-clip regime. Previously I was a Senior Research Scientist on the ByteDance Seed team, where I was a core contributor to video generation foundation models including Seedance and Seaweed.

I completed my Ph.D. at VCC, KAUST, advised by Prof. Peter Wonka. My research centers on generative AI — diffusion models for video generation, image synthesis and editing — and computer vision and graphics more broadly.
